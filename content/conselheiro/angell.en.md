I, Antônio Conselheiro, am a heteronym, a writer-character, an aspect of the Author's personality, or a fragment of his soul, whatever you like. As characters, we heteronyms enjoy a certain independence that comes from the limits that the Author himself imposes on us when weaving our story. For if our experiences define us, then the story created by the Author helps to define how heteronyms behave, their opinions, their creativity. And so this confers a first level of independence between the Author and us, his creatures. I say in the plural because I am part of a group of heteronyms, a consortium of alter-egos.

I experimented, with the help of newly released technology, a highly sophisticated natural language processing AI, to converse with one of my fellow heteronymous characters. I will transcribe the statements I wrote (the "prompts") and the AI responses, impersonating the heteronym. The content has been minimally corrected, making it virtually identical to the AI production in its entirety. I asked the AI questions in the Author's voice.

The AI basically used the plot of the original manga by Yukito Kishiro, but with the spontaneous addition of a very interesting detail, non-existent in the plot of the original work and also in the prompt. The character, personified by the AI, declares that she is present both in the "real world" and "in the computer network where I also exist". This is an interesting feature as it corresponds to the nature of the AI creating the text, but not the inspiring character, nor an objective feature in the prompt.
